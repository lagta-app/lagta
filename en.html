How it works

Set your rules once, and let Lagta grab the right ride, fast.

Set your rules. Minimum fare, and the maximum distance or time to reach the rider.
Turn on auto-accept. Lagta watches offers in the driver app you use.
Grab the ride. When an offer matches your rules, it taps Accept for you in moments.
